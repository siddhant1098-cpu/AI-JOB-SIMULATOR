SPEAKER 1: THE PROBLEM, OUR SOLUTION & FRONTEND (0:00 – 1:30)
================================================================================
[Tech Mentioned: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS]
[Speaker Tip: Focus on how stressful real interviews are, and how our UI fixes that.]

[Slide 1: Title Slide — AI Job Simulator]
"Hello everyone!

Most job interviews and technical tests today have two big problems:
First, they feel robotic and stressful. Candidates get judged by rigid checklists 
that don't reflect how they actually talk or solve problems in real life.
Second, remote tests have a major cheating problem — people easily copy-paste 
answers or look up solutions on their phones.

[Slide 2: Problem & Vision]
To fix this, we created the AI Job Simulator. 
It’s an interactive platform where candidates practice real workplace situations 
by actually speaking, while an on-device smart proctor ensures complete honesty.

I built the Frontend for our app:
- We used React and Vite to make the entire interface super fast and smooth, with 
  no annoying page reloads between questions.
- For design, we used Tailwind CSS with a minimal pastel theme — soft cream, mint, 
  and lavender colors. This creates a calm, welcoming environment that lowers 
  interview anxiety and helps people perform at their best.

Now, my teammate will explain how our Backend, AI, and Voice system work!"

[CUE: Hand over to Speaker 2]

================================================================================
SPEAKER 2: BACKEND, AI INTELLIGENCE & SPEECH (1:30 – 3:00)
================================================================================
[Tech Mentioned: FastAPI, Google Gemini, Web Speech API]
[Speaker Tip: Focus on the AI brain, the warm voice, and fair grading.]

[Slide 3: System Architecture]
"Thank you! Behind the clean frontend, we built a fast and reliable backend.

- We developed our API using FastAPI in Python. It's lightweight, handles multiple 
  requests at once, and connects smoothly with our frontend.

[Slide 4: Conversational AI & Empathetic Grading]
- The brain of our simulator is Google Gemini. Instead of static questions, Gemini 
  creates realistic, role-specific workplace challenges on the spot. 
- Most importantly, we tuned the AI to grade like a friendly mentor. If you explain 
  things in simple, practical human words, it gives you credit. It values genuine 
  problem-solving rather than penalizing everyday conversational English.
- For speech, we used the browser's built-in Web Speech tools:
  - The AI speaks aloud using a natural, relaxed voice so it feels like a real coworker.
  - The candidate can speak their answer directly through the microphone, and their 
    words appear live on screen so they can edit or review before submitting.

Now, my teammate will demonstrate the app live and show our smart anti-cheat system!"

[CUE: Hand over to Speaker 3]

"Thanks! Let's see the simulator in action.

Here on our setup screen, you can choose any role — like Software Engineer or Event

   blocks it instantly and shows a warning.
2. Tab-Switch Alert: If I switch tabs to search for answers, it flags the attempt.
3. Mobile Phone Detection: 
   [Action: Hold your smartphone up in front of the webcam]

   [Action: Put phone down away from camera]
   Once the phone is put away, the screen clears and the test resumes smoothly.

Best of all, this AI vision runs 100% inside your browser using Google MediaPipe. 
No camera video is ever uploaded or recorded to the cloud, protecting candidate privacy.

SPEAKER 4: REPORT, ARCHITECTURE & 1-CLICK LAUNCH (4:30 – 6:00)
================================================================================
[Tech Mentioned: Recharts Data Visualization, LocalStorage, start.bat]
[Speaker Tip: Focus on the encouraging score report, auto-save, and easy setup.]

[Slide 6: Performance Report & Feedback]
"Thank you! After finishing the simulation, the candidate receives an encouraging, 
in-depth performance report.

- We used Recharts to build clean visual charts. Candidates can easily see their 
  strengths across three clear pillars: Practical Solution, Problem Solving, and 
  Communication.
- Instead of a cold rejection score, the report provides warm, actionable feedback: 
  highlighting 3 real strengths and 2 gentle tips to help them grow.
- Employers also get a transparent Proctor Audit summary confirming that the session 
  was clean and honest.

[Slide 7: Zero-Database Simplicity & Roadmap]
From an engineering standpoint, we made the project reliable and easy to share:
- Auto-Save with LocalStorage: We didn't need a complex database. Everything saves 
  in the browser's local storage. If your browser closes accidentally, you can 
  resume right where you left off with zero data loss.
- 1-Click Launch: We created a single `start.bat` file. Anyone can clone our GitHub 
  repository, double-click that file, and it automatically starts both the backend 
  and frontend in seconds.

In summary, the AI Job Simulator makes hiring assessments human, helpful, and 
cheat-proof. 

Thank you, and we'd love to take your questions!"
