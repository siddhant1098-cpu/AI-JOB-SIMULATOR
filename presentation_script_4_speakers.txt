SPEAKER 1: THE HOOK, PROBLEM & OUR SOLUTION (0:00 – 2:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Slide 1: Title Slide — AI Job Simulator]
"Good morning everyone! Have you ever walked out of a job interview or workplace 
test feeling like you were judged by a rigid, cold checklist that didn't capture 
how you actually think or communicate? 

Traditional assessments today have two major problems:
First, they feel robotic. Candidates are bombarded with multiple-choice questions 
or overly harsh AI grading that penalizes everyday spoken language and common sense.
Second, online assessments struggle with integrity — remote candidates often rely 
on mobile phones or copy-paste shortcuts behind the screen.

[Slide 2: The Vision & Solution]
To solve this, our team built the AI Job Simulator — a human-centered, real-time 
simulation platform where candidates practice both realistic job interviews and 
workplace crisis scenarios.

Our simulator is built around three core pillars:
1. Natural Conversational Experience: Instead of reading text off a screen, candidates 
   listen to a natural human voice and speak their answers naturally.
2. Empathetic Mentorship: Our grading respects practical human thinking and composure, 
   offering constructive coaching rather than harsh criticism.
3. On-Device Strict Proctoring: We built an anti-cheat engine directly inside the browser 
   that detects mobile phones and blocks copy-paste shortcuts — without invading 
   privacy or storing video feeds in the cloud.

Now, my teammate will explain how we engineered this under the hood."

SPEAKER 2: ARCHITECTURE, TECH STACK & AI ENGINE (2:00 – 4:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Slide 3: System Architecture & Tech Stack]
"Thank you! Let's take a look at the technical architecture that powers our simulator.

We designed a lightweight, high-performance stack with zero database complexity:
- On the Frontend, we use React 19 and Vite with Tailwind CSS v4. We designed a 
  minimalist, pastel-themed interface using soft alabaster and sage tones to reduce 
  candidate anxiety and create a premium, calm environment.
- On the Backend, we built an asynchronous REST API using Python and FastAPI, 
  powered by Pydantic v2 schemas for strict contract validation.

[Slide 4: Conversational AI & Empathetic Grading]
The core intelligence runs on Google Gemini 3.5 Flash through the Google AI Studio API. 
We selected Gemini Flash because it delivers sub-second response times, allowing 
realistic scenarios to be generated and scored on the fly. 

To ensure zero downtime during high traffic, we implemented an automatic failover 
mechanism: if the primary model hits any latency, it instantly switches to 
gemini-3.5-flash-lite. We also integrated `json-repair` to dynamically sanitize 
all JSON responses, eliminating parsing errors.

For speech, we tapped into the browser's native Web Speech API:
- Our Text-to-Speech engine automatically selects high-fidelity natural human voices 
  and speaks at a comfortable 0.95x cadence, so the simulator feels like a supportive 
  colleague having a conversation over coffee.
- Our Speech-to-Text transcribes the candidate's words in real-time, displaying a 
  live editable preview so any speech recognition quirks can be polished before submission.

Now, my teammate will walk you through a live demonstration of the simulator in action!"

SPEAKER 3: LIVE DEMO & REAL-TIME PROCTORING (4:00 – 6:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Slide 5: Live Demonstration & Strict Proctoring]
[Action: Open http://localhost:5173 on screen]
"Thanks! Let's see the simulator in action.

Here on our setup screen, a candidate can select any domain — like Event Management, 
Software Engineering, or Operations — choose their company size, and pick between 
an Interview or a Workplace Scenario. Notice our 'Strict Anti-Cheat Proctor' toggle. 
Let's start the simulation!

[Action: Click 'Start Assessment'. The question card appears and TTS reads aloud.]
Notice how the question is read aloud in a warm, human tone. The candidate doesn't 
feel like they're taking an exam; they feel like they're talking to a colleague.

Now, let's talk about our proctoring innovations:
[Action: Try selecting or right-clicking text, or press Ctrl+C]
First: Notice that text selection and right-click are completely blocked. If I press 
Ctrl+C or attempt to paste into the answer box, an instant red warning appears: 
'Pasting is prohibited in Strict Mode.'

Second: Tab-switching detection. If a candidate attempts to switch to another tab 
to Google the answer, the screen instantly blurs and logs a timestamped violation.

Third — and most exciting — is our AI Mobile Phone Detection:
[Action: Hold up your smartphone in front of the camera]
Look at the camera preview in the bottom corner. As soon as I hold up my mobile phone, 
the system identifies it as a handheld device, the screen immediately blurs with 
'Mobile Device Detected!', and the assessment timer pauses! 

[Action: Put the phone down away from camera]
The moment the phone is put away, the screen unblurs automatically! 

What makes this technically remarkable is that this entire vision pipeline is powered 
by Google's MediaPipe Tasks Vision running on-device using WebAssembly and WebGL. 
We run BlazeFace for face tracking and EfficientDet-Lite0 for object detection. 
Not a single frame of video is sent to the cloud, guaranteeing complete user privacy.

Now, my teammate will show you the final debrief and our future roadmap."

SPEAKER 4: FINAL DEBRIEF, ARCHITECTURE & ROADMAP (6:00 – 8:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Slide 6: Performance Report & Proctor Audit]
"Thank you! Once the candidate finishes their simulation, they receive this comprehensive, 
encouraging Performance Debrief.

Notice the key features here:
1. Balanced Scoring: Candidates are marked on Practical Solution (40%), Problem 
   Solving (30%), and Communication (30%). Our prompts evaluate practical common sense 
   and empathy, giving candidates realistic, supportive scores rather than harsh penalties.
2. Visual Analytics: Powered by Recharts, our interactive Radar and Bar charts help 
   candidates visually understand their competencies at a glance.
3. Mentorship Insights: Instead of generic scores, the candidate receives 3 specific 
   strengths and 3 actionable development steps.
4. Proctor Audit Log: In the proctor card, interviewers can see a categorized breakdown 
   of all events — mobile phone detections, clipboard locks, and tab switches — giving 
   recruiters complete confidence in the integrity of the test.

[Slide 7: Zero-Database Architecture & Scalability]
From an engineering standpoint, we eliminated database overhead completely:
- The entire session state, questions, transcripts, and integrity logs persist in the 
  browser's localStorage with timestamp recovery. If a candidate's browser refreshes, 
  they can resume right where they left off.
- To make sharing effortless, we created an automated 1-click startup script that 
  auto-installs npm and pip dependencies on any machine.

[Slide 8: Conclusion & Future Roadmap]
Looking ahead, our roadmap includes:
- Company-specific custom knowledge bases so organizations can simulate their exact workplace tools.
- Multilingual simulation support for global recruiting.
- Seamless integrations with ATS platforms like Greenhouse and Workday.

Thank you so much for your time today! We are now open for any questions."
