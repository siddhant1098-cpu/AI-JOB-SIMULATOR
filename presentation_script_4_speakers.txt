SPEAKER 1: THE HOOK, PROBLEM, SOLUTION & FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE (0:00 – 2:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Tech Focus for Speaker 1: React 19, Vite v8, Tailwind CSS v4, Plus Jakarta Sans]

[Slide 1: Title Slide — AI Job Simulator]
"Good morning everyone! Have you ever walked out of a job interview or workplace 
test feeling like you were judged by a rigid, cold checklist that didn't capture 
how you actually think, communicate, or handle pressure?

Traditional technical and workplace assessments today suffer from two critical flaws:
First, they feel robotic. Candidates are tested through static multiple-choice 
quizzes or overly harsh AI grading that penalizes everyday spoken language and 
real-world common sense.
Second, remote assessments face severe integrity challenges — candidates frequently 
turn to mobile phones or copy-pasting solutions behind the camera.

[Slide 2: The Vision & Solution]
To solve this, our team engineered the AI Job Simulator — a human-centered, 
real-time simulation platform where candidates engage in authentic conversational 
interviews and dynamic workplace crisis scenarios.

Our platform is founded on three distinct pillars:
1. Natural Spoken Dialogue: Candidates listen to an authentic voice and reply 
   naturally through speech, exactly like a real workplace conversation.
2. Empathetic Mentorship: Our evaluation engine values practical initiative, 
   calm judgment, and problem-solving, offering encouraging coaching rather 
   than harsh penalties.
3. 100% On-Device Anti-Cheat: A zero-cloud computer vision engine that detects 
   smartphones and enforces clipboard security right inside the browser.

To bring this vision to life, I led the Frontend Architecture:
- We built the client with React 19, structuring the app as a modular state machine 
  transitioning smoothly across setup, active simulation, real-time feedback, and 
  the final report without disruptive page reloads.
- We paired React with Vite v8 as our build engine, giving us sub-second Hot Module 
  Replacement and a built-in reverse proxy that routes frontend API calls directly 
  to our backend without CORS issues.
- For styling, we utilized Tailwind CSS v4 to design a custom Minimal Pastel theme. 
  By combining soft alabaster backgrounds (#FAF8F5), delicate lavender and sage tones, 
  and Plus Jakarta Sans typography, our UI actively lowers candidate anxiety and 
  creates a calm, premium assessment atmosphere.

Now, I'll pass it over to my teammate to explain our Backend, AI, and Speech pipeline!"

SPEAKER 2: BACKEND ARCHITECTURE, AI REASONING & CONVERSATIONAL SPEECH (2:00 – 4:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Tech Focus for Speaker 2: FastAPI, Uvicorn, Pydantic v2, Google Gemini 3.5 Flash, 
                          Dual-Model Fallback, json-repair, Web Speech API]

[Slide 3: System Architecture & Tech Stack]
"Thank you! Behind our calming pastel frontend lies a high-throughput, asynchronous 
backend engineered for speed, resilience, and natural conversation.

Let's examine the core backend and intelligence layer:
- We developed our REST API using Python and FastAPI, running on the asynchronous 
  Uvicorn ASGI server. FastAPI was chosen for its high concurrency and native 
  integration with Pydantic v2, which strictly validates all candidate configuration, 
  question payloads, and evaluation schemas at the network boundary.

[Slide 4: Conversational AI & Empathetic Grading]
The core intelligence driving our simulator is Google Gemini 3.5 Flash via the 
Google AI Studio API. We chose Gemini Flash because of its sub-second token latency, 
enabling the platform to generate unique, role-specific workplace challenges and 
evaluate spoken answers on the fly.

To guarantee 100% platform reliability, we implemented two critical fault-tolerant 
mechanisms:
1. Dual-Model Auto-Failover: If the primary Gemini 3.5 Flash model experiences any 
   free-tier rate limiting or network congestion, our backend automatically fails over 
   to gemini-3.5-flash-lite in milliseconds, ensuring the candidate never sees a crash.
2. AST Syntax Healing with json-repair: Large language models occasionally produce 
   unescaped quotation marks when generating realistic workplace dialogue. We integrated 
   the `json-repair` library in our parsing pipeline to automatically heal and sanitize 
   JSON responses in memory before they reach the client.

For the conversational audio experience, we harnessed the browser's native Web Speech API:
- Our Text-to-Speech engine uses `SpeechSynthesis`, custom-tuned to a relaxed 0.95x 
  speaking cadence. We programmed it to auto-detect and select the highest-fidelity 
  human voices available — like Microsoft Natural Online or Google US English — so 
  it sounds like a warm, supportive colleague having coffee with you.
- On the input side, our Speech-to-Text engine uses `webkitSpeechRecognition` to transcribe 
  the candidate's answer continuously in real-time, displaying a live editable text area 
  so candidates can correct any microphone transcription quirks before submitting.

Now, my teammate will demonstrate the simulator and our on-device vision proctor live!"

SPEAKER 3: LIVE DEMO, COMPUTER VISION & ON-DEVICE ANTI-CHEAT (4:00 – 6:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Tech Focus for Speaker 3: MediaPipe Tasks Vision (WASM), MediaPipe BlazeFace, 
                          MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite0, HTML5 Canvas Luminance, 
                          DOM Event & Clipboard Security]

[Slide 5: Live Demonstration & Strict Proctoring]
[Action: Open http://localhost:5173 on screen]
"Thanks! Let's see the AI Job Simulator live in action.

Here on our setup screen, a candidate can select any domain — such as Event Management 
or Software Engineering — choose their company type, and pick between an Interview or 
a Workplace Challenge. Notice our 'Strict Anti-Cheat Proctor' toggle. Let's start!

[Action: Click 'Start Assessment'. Question card appears and TTS reads aloud.]
Notice how the question is read aloud with natural conversational phrasing: 
'Hello! Let's start with our first scenario...' The candidate is invited into an authentic 
conversation rather than an intimidating test.

Now, let's explore our breakthrough feature: On-Device Strict Proctoring.
[Action: Try selecting or right-clicking text, or press Ctrl+C]
First — Clipboard and Text Security:
Notice that text selection is completely disabled. If I try to right-click or press 
Ctrl+C, a red warning toast pops up: 'Keyboard shortcut [Ctrl+C] is disabled.' 
If I attempt to paste an external answer into the response field, our DOM listeners 
suppress the event immediately and log a clipboard violation flag.

Second — Tab-Switch Monitoring:
If a candidate tries to navigate to another tab to Google the answer, our 
`document.visibilitychange` listener instantly triggers a blurred overlay and logs 
a timestamped event.

Third — and our proudest engineering achievement — AI Mobile Phone Detection:
[Action: Hold up your smartphone in front of the webcam]
Look at the camera preview in the bottom corner. The moment my mobile phone enters 
the camera frame, the badge flashes '📱 PHONE!', the screen immediately freezes with a 
heavy 25px blur overlay, and the timer pauses!

[Action: Put the smartphone down away from camera]
As soon as the phone is put away, the overlay clears and the test resumes seamlessly!

Here is the technical magic:
This entire computer vision system is powered by Google's MediaPipe Tasks Vision, 
running 100% on the client device through WebAssembly and WebGL:
- For facial verification, we run MediaPipe BlazeFace every 500 milliseconds, checking 
  bounding boxes to verify single-candidate presence and flag turned-away faces.
- For mobile detection, we run MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite0, an on-device object 
  detection neural network trained on the COCO dataset that identifies handheld devices.
- For physical camera tampering, a background HTML5 Canvas analyzes pixel luminance to 
  catch covered lenses.
Most importantly: Not a single video frame is ever recorded or uploaded to the cloud, 
giving candidates complete biometric privacy while giving employers total exam integrity!

Now, my teammate will walk you through our final debrief and architecture highlights."

SPEAKER 4: VISUAL ANALYTICS, ZERO-DATABASE ARCHITECTURE & ROADMAP (6:00 – 8:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Tech Focus for Speaker 4: Recharts Data Visualization, Deterministic Rubric Engine, 
                          Browser localStorage State Recovery, 1-Click start.bat]

[Slide 6: Performance Report & Proctor Audit]
"Thank you! Once the candidate finishes the simulation, they receive this comprehensive, 
encouraging Performance Debrief.

Let's look at the data analytics and scoring architecture:
1. Deterministic Weighted Rubric: We score every answer across Practical Solution (40%), 
   Problem Solving (30%), and Communication (30%). Crucially, the weighted total is 
   computed in deterministic code rather than LLM approximation, guaranteeing mathematical 
   precision.
2. Interactive Visualizations with Recharts: We integrated Recharts to render an interactive 
   SVG Competency Radar Chart and Performance Bar Chart, allowing candidates to visualize 
   their strengths across all three dimensions at a glance.
3. Empathetic Coaching Debrief: Rather than cold percentages, the report delivers 3 authentic 
   strengths, 2 gentle growth areas, and 3 actionable development steps.
4. Proctor Audit Trail: Interviewers receive a transparent breakdown of all proctor observations — 
   displaying exact counts of Mobile Phone detections, Clipboard lock attempts, Tab switches, 
   and Face checks, accompanied by a 'Verified Clean' badge.

[Slide 7: Zero-Database Architecture & Scalability]
From an infrastructure standpoint, we solved deployment complexity with two innovations:
- Stateless Backend & localStorage Persistence: We deliberately eliminated database 
  overhead. The entire simulation state, question sets, speech transcripts, and audit logs 
  persist in browser `localStorage`. If a candidate accidentally reloads their browser, 
  our timestamp-recovery engine presents a 1-click 'Resume Session' screen, seamlessly 
  restoring the interview without data loss.
- 1-Click Plug-and-Play Deployment (`start.bat`): To make sharing frictionless, we built an 
  automated launcher script. When cloned on any new computer, it automatically verifies 
  Python and Node.js environments, installs missing npm and pip packages, creates the .env 
  configuration, and boots both servers in one click.

[Slide 8: Conclusion & Future Roadmap]
Looking toward the future, our roadmap includes:
- Organization-specific knowledge base integration for custom workplace crisis simulations.
- Real-time multilingual simulation support for international recruitment.
- Enterprise ATS webhook integrations with platforms like Greenhouse and Workday.

The AI Job Simulator proves that technical assessments can be rigorous and secure 
without sacrificing human empathy and privacy.

Thank you so much! We are now excited to answer any questions."
